# Privacy Policy for Sonogram: Sound Puzzle

**Last updated: 30 October 2026**

Sonogram: Sound Puzzle is a mobile game developed and published by Moss Dug.

We respect your privacy and aim to keep data collection to a minimum.

## 1. Information We Collect

Moss Dug does not directly collect or maintain a database of players' personal information.

Sonogram stores gameplay progress, preferences, and settings locally on your device. Certain information may also be processed or stored by Google services described below.

## 2. Advertising

Sonogram uses Google AdMob to display optional rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, and advertisement interaction data for advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

Where required, players are offered advertising privacy and consent choices.

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. Google Play Games Services

Sonogram may use Google Play Games Services to support achievements, player progress, and cloud save functionality.

When these features are enabled, Google may process information including player identifiers, achievement records, and saved game progress.

This information is handled through Google's services and subject to Google's privacy practices.

## 4. In-App Purchases

Sonogram offers optional purchases through Google Play Billing.

Google Play processes payments and maintains purchase records, including transaction details and purchase ownership information.

Sonogram may access purchase status and transaction identifiers to unlock purchased content and restore purchases.

Moss Dug does not receive or store your payment card details.

## 5. Data Storage

Gameplay progress and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Where Google Play Games cloud saving is enabled, supported progress may also be stored through Google's services.

Uninstalling Sonogram may delete locally stored information. Cloud-stored information and purchase records may remain available through your Google account.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Moss Dug does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

## 7. Third-Party Services

Sonogram uses or plans to use:

- Google AdMob, for optional rewarded advertisements.
- Google Play Billing, for in-app purchases.
- Google Play Games Services, for achievements and cloud saves.

For information about Google's data practices, visit [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Sonogram's features or data practices change.

Changes will be published here with an updated revision date.
